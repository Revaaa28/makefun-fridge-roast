## The line

> My smart fridge has a password. I forgot the password. I am now locked out of my own leftovers.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else runs; no network, no secrets.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## How the line was chosen

Twelve candidates were written and ranked by hand against the judge brief, in the brief's
own order: roast of the fridge itself, punchline lands on first read, specific to smart
fridges, tight wording, fresh angle (no ordering-groceries or watching-you cracks).
See [candidates.md](candidates.md) for the full list and the ranking.
